# C023R inherited metadata retention of erased forcing-witness bits — prereg v1.0

Date: 2026-09-15
Authority: `HQ_CAPTAIN_OBVIOUS_SYMBOLIC_PREREG__NO_SCIENTIFIC_PROMOTION`

## Parent theorem

`HISTORY_CHARGE_SEMANTIC_ERASURE_BARRIER_2026-09-15.md`

establishes that expansion-forced history charge is not source-residual memory. For a fixed forced MAJ3 output, three distinct equal-coordinate forcing-pair witnesses collapse to the same source-semantic residual. Hence any positive cache-fibre injectivity theorem must rely on execution metadata beyond the restricted source relation.

## Exact target

Determine whether the historical Policy-0A transition retains erased forcing-witness identity through inherited non-source clauses strongly enough to recover all but `o(L)` independent erased history bits on the frozen q=4 family.

The gate is intentionally split into a one-bit base test before any asymptotic composition claim.

## G1 — one-bit retention cell

Fix one MAJ3 block `(a,b,c)` and a target output `z`.

Consider two reachable histories `h,h'` whose source-semantic restrictions agree and which differ only in the forcing-pair witness used to force this block to `z`, e.g. for `z=0`:

- `h`: `a=0,b=0`,
- `h'`: `a=0,c=0`,

with all other source-semantic restrictions matched.

A **retention witness** is an explicit inherited non-source clause or deterministic transition difference that remains present after the block has become source-semantically constant and distinguishes the two histories in the byte-level cache key or forces distinct future keys.

A **forgetting witness** is an exact pair of reachable histories satisfying the above conditions that reconverge to one byte-identical cache key after the forcing block is semantically eliminated, including every inherited clause and all frozen UP/Resolution effects.

## G2 — exact origin of any retention witness

Because complete source-only pivots are already proved stifling-transparent, any retention witness must trace to at least one of:

1. a budget-truncated partial-pivot addition prefix;
2. a Resolution inference involving an already inherited non-source clause;
3. a cross-factor accepted resolvent enabled by inherited clause geometry;
4. deterministic UP/branch effects caused by 1–3.

The proof must identify the exact origin. Merely observing that two historical contexts differ is insufficient.

## G3 — persistence

If a one-bit retention witness exists, prove exactly how long it survives restriction, canonical deduplication, future local Resolution and unit propagation.

A clause that is immediately satisfied/dropped or becomes a duplicate of the common source residual does not count as retained information.

## G4 — composition

Only after G1–G3 are solved may the gate ask whether `r` independent forcing-witness bits can produce `r-o(L)` independently recoverable metadata bits or, conversely, whether `Omega(L)` erased bits can coexist with one byte-identical key.

No independence assumption may be inferred from disjoint source blocks alone; inherited clauses may couple their causal cones.

## Killer falsifier first

The cheapest decisive falsifier for the positive fingerprint route is:

`ONE_REACHABLE_FORCING_WITNESS_BIT_FORGOTTEN_EXACTLY`

— one exact reachable pair of histories, differing in one forcing-pair witness but otherwise source-semantically matched, that reconverges including inherited metadata.

This alone does not prove an exponential fibre. It falsifies any claim that every charged forcing witness is individually recoverable and forces a weaker aggregate retention theorem.

## Positive base outcome

The strongest allowed one-bit PASS is:

`ONE_BIT_RETENTION_WITNESS_PROVED_FOR_DECLARED_REACHABLE_CELL`

This does not imply linear composability.

## Forbidden

- finite-instance frequency extrapolation;
- K4/K3,3 as asymptotic evidence;
- source-semantic equality substituted for byte-key equality;
- ignoring inherited clauses;
- structural renaming;
- changing branch order, pivot order, budgets or encoding;
- assuming all three forcing-pair witnesses are reachable;
- promoting a one-bit result to `2^{o(L)}` without G4.

## First allowed action

Derive a transition-level persistence criterion for one candidate inherited clause under a forcing-pair swap. This must be symbolic and must state exact conditions under which the clause is dropped, duplicated or remains distinguishing.

## Claim ceiling

No Policy-0A lower bound, no C022 H137 promotion, no SAT/P-vs-NP conclusion.
