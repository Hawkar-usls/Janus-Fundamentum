# R5 E8 6I — Effective Short pp-Definitions in the Residually-Small Cube-Term Regime

**Status:** Candidate theorem manuscript, v0.1 — end-to-end mathematical audit passed; theorem seal withheld pending standalone proof verification and independent prior-art/novelty review.

## 1. Candidate theorem

Fix a finite family K of finite algebras with a common cube term and assume V(K) is residually small. Fix a finite multi-sorted relational basis Gamma for the finite factor universe used in the Bulín–Kompatscher residual-finite proof.

Given generators (equivalently, after polynomial preprocessing, a standardized compact representation) of an invariant relation

R <= A_1 x ... x A_n,   A_i in HS(K),

construct in deterministic polynomial time a pp-formula phi_R over Gamma defining R. The candidate output bound is

|phi_R| = O(n^max(2,k-1))

for fixed edge/cube parameter k and fixed K.

This manuscript does **not** claim a theorem until every derived lemma below is checked independently.

## 3. Constructive decomposition

Let q0=max(k,3) for the algorithmic shell used by the BMS localization steps. Build the fixed-arity low-projection shell from the projections needed by the edge representation and BMS small-projection checks.

Initialize C to the shell relation.

While C != R:
1. Obtain y in C\R using polynomial compact-representation comparison.
2. Run the pointed maximal-separator algorithm for (R,y).
3. Delete dummy coordinates constructively.
4. Obtain a critical parallelogram separator S.
5. Replace C by C intersect S.

The source signature argument bounds the number of nonredundant critical conjuncts by O(n).

## 4. Lemma A — SAFEEXT/maximal-separator equivalence

For invariant S and y notin S define SAFEEXT(S,y) as finding t notin S with

y notin Sg(S union {t}),

or certifying that no such t exists.

If no such t exists, every strict invariant superrelation of S contains y, hence Sg(S union {y}) is the unique cover of S. Therefore S is completely meet-irreducible and y is a key tuple.

Conversely, a pointed maximal-separator routine yields a SAFEEXT witness by extracting any tuple from a strict maximal extension.

## 5. Lemma B — Old-skeleton cross-fibre affine avoidance

This replaces the earlier overly narrow “same-rho-fibre” statement.

Let q be an old quotient-skeleton class containing a new tuple t and let q_y be the old class of the target y. Put

G_q=o_q/rho, K_q=B cap G_q, M_q=G_q/K_q,
G_y=o_y/rho, K_y=B cap G_y, M_y=G_y/K_y.

For every Algorithm-8 unary polynomial type p mapping q to q_y, Theorem 2.2 gives an affine restriction whose linear part descends to a homomorphism

Lbar_p : M_q -> M_y.

Claim 6.7 decomposes target membership into the subgroup generated by these generator contributions. Since the number of polynomial types and local group exponents are constants depending only on fixed K, all additive coefficient combinations yield a fixed finite family

Lambda_(q->y) subset Hom(M_q,M_y)

such that for v=t-o_q mod K_q,

y in Sg(B,t)
iff
exists L in Lambda_(q->y): L(v)=ybar.

Hence the unsafe set is a constant union of affine fibres. Decision and witness construction are polynomial via finite-abelian-group linear algebra plus constant-size inclusion-exclusion/self-reduction.

**Formal proof obligation:** spell out the coefficient domain and verify that every subgroup combination in Claim 6.7 is represented by one member of Lambda_(q->y), and conversely.

## 6. Lemma C — Quotient-changing SAFEEXT

Assume a globally safe quotient-changing t exists. Apply the BMS structure/localization theorem to C=Sg(B,t) after all required small projections have passed. Failure of y-membership localizes to a large post-extension coherence block U.

Inside U, the quotient-pair skeleton of B_U equals that of C_U: B_U is a subdirect subset of each quotient graph and therefore must equal the full graph. Thus t|U belongs to an **old** quotient-skeleton class q. Lemma B applies, regardless of whether q=q_y.

Use post-extension block refinement and original-bundle-slice integrity to construct a liftable safe projection/witness and then a full original tuple whose safety is certified by projection onto U (or the corresponding one-bundle complement used in the normalization proof).

**Formal proof obligation:** write the lift-back diagram explicitly and verify the exact relation between W-coordinates, original bundles W_j, and the final original-coordinate tuple.

## 7. Lemma D — Constructive dummy deletion

Let S be maximal excluding y. Coordinate i is essential iff there exists a in A_i such that y with coordinate i replaced by a lies in S.

If no such neighbour exists, y is outside the cylinder proj_-i(S) x A_i. This cylinder is an invariant superrelation of S; maximality forces equality, hence i is dummy. The converse follows immediately from the cylinder property.

Membership tests are polynomial using the compact/SMP calculus; each domain is fixed finite.

## 8. Critical separator and outer decomposition

After dummy deletion, the maximal separator is critical. Because y passed the frozen low-arity shell, its essential arity is at least the threshold needed for the source parallelogram theorem. Therefore the separator has the parallelogram property.

The source signature-drop argument bounds nonredundant critical conjuncts by at most n|A|^2 in the single-sorted fixed-domain notation, with the corresponding fixed constant for the multisorted factor universe.

## 9. Nonreduced -> reduced

For a critical subdirect parallelogram relation S, compute coordinate linkedness congruences theta_i by compact product/equality/projection operations.

Map compact generators through

S -> S/(theta_1,...,theta_n).

The quotient relation is reduced, critical, subdirect and parallelogram; S is its full inverse image. Recompact the image through SMP(HS(K)) <->_P CompactRep(HS(K)).

## 10. Reduced critical recursion

For reduced critical R define

A_12=proj_12(R)/theta_12,

Q(x1,x2,z) iff z=(x1,x2)/theta_12,

R'=h(R),

h(x1,x2,x3,...,xn)=([(x1,x2)]_theta12,x3,...,xn).

If F_R generates R then h(F_R) generates R'. Recompact h(F_R) polynomially over HS(K). The arity drops from n to n-1. The quotient algebra is SI and belongs to the fixed finite factor universe, so Q belongs to the fixed ternary basis.

Recurse to arity <=3.
