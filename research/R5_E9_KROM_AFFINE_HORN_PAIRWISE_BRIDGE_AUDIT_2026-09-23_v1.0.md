## 1. Positive bridge: Krom × affine when the affine boundary is 2-affine

Let (X={x_1,ldots,x_n}) be a live boundary.

Assume:
- (K(X)) is represented by a 2-CNF (bijunctive/Krom) summary;
- (A(X)) is an affine relation represented by (Mx=b) over (mathbb F_2).

Call (A) **2-affine** iff it can be defined by unary/binary parity equations:
[
x_i=c,qquad x_ioplus x_j=c.
]

This matches the standard notion: 2-affine relations are exactly affine relations with width at most two; equivalently they are both affine and bijunctive.

1. recognizes whether the affine boundary relation (A) is 2-affine;
2. if YES, constructs an equivalent polynomial-size 2-CNF;
