## 1. Candidate potentials being falsified

The strategic replay listed natural ingredients such as:

- cycle/connectivity rank of the nonlinear product structure;
- dimension of the affine row space modulo its weight<=2-generated part;
- lexicographic combinations of a nonlinear-layer complexity and an
  affine-width complexity.

These are useful diagnostics, but the exact two-path normal form gives a
universal negative control for any potential that only sees the two layers
separately.

## 2. Product-layer value

In the two-path normal form every clause contributes only

    t_C = a_1 a_2
    u_C = t_C a_3
    u_C = 0.

Different clause product variables are private.

Therefore the product-factor layer is a disjoint union of constant-length
paths/trees.

Hence its ordinary graph cycle rank is zero.

Any invariant that vanishes on forests, when applied only to the product
layer, is already at its minimum on every transformed arbitrary 3CNF.

## 3. Affine-layer value

All global coherence equations are unary pinnings and XOR2 path equations

    a_i + a_{i+1} = c_i + c_{i+1}.

Thus the entire affine row space is generated by rows of Hamming weight at
most two (plus constant/unary rows in the augmented representation).

Consequently

    dim(
      affine rowspace
      /
      span(weight<=2 affine rows)
    )
    =
    0.

The high-width-affine quotient defect is also already at its minimum.

## 4. Exact universal coexistence

The same representation is an exact linear-size reduction of arbitrary signed
3CNF.

Therefore arbitrary 3SAT is represented while simultaneously

    product cycle rank = 0

and

    high-width affine quotient dimension = 0.

This is an expressiveness theorem, not a P-versus-NP lower bound.

## 5. Consequence

No proposed progress measure of the form

    Phi(I)
    =
    f(
      invariant(product layer alone),
      invariant(affine layer alone)
    )

can certify universal progress if both component invariants take their
terminal/minimal value on the two-path normal form.

In particular the naive lexicographic pair

    (
      product-layer cycle rank,

    (0,0)

Thus a valid joint potential must inspect the CROSS-LAYER OVERLAY itself.
