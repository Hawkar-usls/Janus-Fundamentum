## Actual theorem and corrected expert precondition

Previously the polynomial-calculus odd-automorphism adapter
required that EVERY original row move in a full |G|-length orbit.
This is **NOT** required by the mathematical no-loss theorem and
needlessly prevented certification on inputs with invariant rows
(or shorter odd row orbits).

Let R=F2[x_0,...,x_(n-1)]/(x_i²=x_i), and let W_3 be
the F2-linear span of all bounded-degree-3 multiples
of exact-one constraints

  p_C = 1 + sum_{v in C} x_v,
  q_C = sum_{a<b in C} x_a*x_b.

If `sigma` is a VERIFIED variable permutation that preserves the
original set of exact-one clauses and has odd order h, W3 is sigma
stable. Define the Reynolds-type averaging operator

  P(g) = sum_{t=0}^{h-1} sigma^t(g).

Because h is odd, P(1)=1 and P(W3) belongs to W3^G.
Thus

  1 in W3  <=>  1 in W3^G.

This equivalence holds regardless of the lengths of individual
orbits of variables, clauses, generator multiples, or monomials.
All orbit lengths d divide h and are odd, so the group sum of an
element in an orbit of length d repeats each distinct orbit member
h/d times, which is odd over F2. The sum is consequently the
distinct orbit sum. Invariant monomial orbit sums are a full basis
for R_{<=3}^G, including FIXED monomials (size-1 orbits).

Because the original degree-three W3 is stable under the group,
its invariant span is generated by the orbit averages of ALL
original degree-three generators. Selecting one clause from each
clause orbit and considering all multiplier monomials is sufficient:
any other generator is a group translate of one from a representative
clause. This proves full-orbit-length is unnecessary.

**Restrictions for the current executable:** sigma must be a
bijective original-input column permutation, maps exact source
clauses to exact clauses, has odd order 1<h<=n (bounded by a
fixed polynomial for this implementation), and EVERY row orbit
must have length dividing h; arbitrary row orbit lengths are
accepted even if 1. Discovery of such a sigma is NOT automatically
provided on arbitrary SCL inputs.

ORIGINAL full Boolean polynomial ring, by explicitly expanding
all chosen original row equations, multiplying by the claimed
squarefree monomial and orbiting the generator through all h

using the Gaussian basis, orbit coordinate projection, or the
`poly_row` producer. To declare UNSAT, the resulting raw
