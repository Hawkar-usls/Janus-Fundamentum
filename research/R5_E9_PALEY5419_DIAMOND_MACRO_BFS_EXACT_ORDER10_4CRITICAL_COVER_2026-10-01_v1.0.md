## 2. Diamond implication lemma

Take supported differences `a,b,b-a in S` satisfying the balanced-triangle equation

```text
phi_c(b)-phi_c(a)=phi_c(b-a).
```

Put

```text
T = x+a+b.
```

Select the five coordinate edges

```text
x--(x+a),
x--(x+b),
(x+a)--(x+b),
(x+a)--T,
(x+b)--T.
```

They form `K4-e`, with the nonedge `x--T`.  Define local switching values

```text
g(x)=0,
g(x+a)=phi_c(a),
g(x+b)=phi_c(b),
g(T)=phi_c(a)+phi_c(b).
```

The balanced-triangle equation makes all five gain equations compatible with this switching.
If a potential `p` avoids all five selected hyperplanes, then

```text
q(v)=p(v)-g(v)
```

is a proper 3-colouring of the diamond.  In every proper 3-colouring of `K4-e`, its two degree-two nonadjacent vertices have the same colour.  Therefore

```text
q(T)=q(x),
```

and hence avoidance of the diamond forces the affine endpoint equation

```text
p(T)-p(x)=phi_c(a)+phi_c(b).
```

Thus a balanced diamond is an exact implication macro-edge

```text
(a+b, phi_c(a)+phi_c(b)) in Z_q x F3.
```

No heuristic or assignment sampling is used.
