## 6. Invariance of high Tanner treewidth under matched contraction

by contracting ANY valid Tanner perfect matching.

maximum bag size w+1. Replace each quotient cell v
in each bag by BOTH its original vertices (one row,
one matched variable); lifted bags contain <=2(w+1)
original Tanner vertices.

- Every original matched edge is covered by a bag
  containing its quotient cell.
- Every original unmatched Tanner edge maps to a Q
  edge, hence its endpoints occur together in at
  least one lifted bag.
- Bags containing either original half of a
  contracted cell are exactly the connected
  set of bags containing the quotient cell.
Thus the lifted decomposition is valid for H.

```

tw(Q) >= (tw(H)-1)/2.
```

This refutes any assertion that perfect matching
