## G3 — public source exhaustion

### S1 — Splitter theory preserves N-minors, not a prescribed torso role

Seymour's Splitter Theorem, as summarized in Oxley's *Matroid Theory*, gives
3-connected deletion/contraction steps that preserve an isomorphic copy of a
specified 3-connected minor N.

Strong Splitter variants constrain the allowed extension/coextension sequence.

This is an exact collision with:

```
KEEP THE SPECIFIED N-MINOR ALIVE
WHILE MOVING THROUGH 3-CONNECTED MINORS.
```

It is **not** a theorem that N appears as a selected 1/2/3-sum torso with a
prescribed virtual interface in the original M.

Classification:
`SPECIFIED_MINOR_PRESERVATION_SOURCE_BOUND__TORSO_PLACEMENT_NOT_SUPPLIED`.

### S2 — N-detachable-pair theory is the strongest located placement donor

Brettell--Whittle--Williams study a 3-connected matroid M with a specified
3-connected minor N.  Their N-detachable-pair series gives detailed structure
when two-element deletion/contraction cannot preserve both 3-connectivity and an
N-minor.  The obstruction language includes N-grounded triangles/triads and
special 3-separators such as spike-like, skew-whiff, twisted-cube-like,
elongated-quad, double-quad and related separators.

This is directly relevant to PA-0007 because it couples:

```
specified N-minor
+
3-connectivity
+
where the unavoidable 3-separating structure sits.
```

The papers also use localization facts such as: across a 2-separation of a
matroid with a 3-connected N-minor, one side meets E(N) in at most one element
under the stated hypotheses.

However the series does not prove:

```
EVERY specified N
CAN BE MADE A LITERAL 3-SUM TORSO
OF AN ARBITRARY RESTRICTED PARENT CLASS.
```

Nor does it impose the JANUS cubic parent or the lower-bound-tight shortest-f
objective.

Classification:
`STRONG_SPECIFIED_MINOR_SEPARATOR_DONOR__NO_EXACT_PLACEMENT_COLLISION`.

### S3 — 3-separation tree theory describes a fixed M, not universal embedding

Oxley--Semple--Whittle give a tree decomposition that displays, up to natural
equivalence, the non-trivial 3-separations of a fixed 3-connected matroid.

This is a source-bound language for the phrase "actual decomposition placement".

It does not say that an arbitrary 3-connected minor N of M is itself one of the
displayed torsos/nodes, and it does not construct a cubic parent whose
decomposition has a prescribed N at a prescribed position.

Classification:
`FIXED_PARENT_3SEPARATION_STRUCTURE_SOURCE_BOUND__NO_UNIVERSAL_PLACEMENT`.

### S4 — specified-minor structure can force placement-like minors, but only after another minor operation

Chun--Ding--Mayhew--Oxley prove a strong theorem for sufficiently large connected
matroids retaining a fixed connected minor N.  Up to duality, they obtain a
large connected minor where either:

- N is a spanning restriction with a large connected uniform remainder; or
- there is a minor that is a 2-sum of a big circuit and a connected
  single-element extension/coextension of N.

This is a genuine placement-like donor.

But its scope is different from PA-0007:

- N is fixed in the asymptotic theorem;
- the conclusion is obtained after passing to an additional minor;
- the host is not required to be an exact square cubic `M([I+P+Q|1])`;
- it does not produce the required 3-sum/two-row interface;
- it does not couple a shortest-f threshold.

Classification:
`PLACEMENT_LIKE_FIXED_MINOR_DONOR__ADJACENT_ONLY`.

plain minor-image, cubicity, S8, connectivity, two-row form, or scalar separator
weights are already closed as standalone currencies.
