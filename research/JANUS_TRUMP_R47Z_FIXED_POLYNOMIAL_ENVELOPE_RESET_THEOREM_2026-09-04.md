## Motivation

R47V used the root clause count `C0` itself as a reset cap. R47X produced an explicit reachable state showing that this exact cap is too tight for the frozen R47M projection grammar.

That refutes `C <= C0` as a universal invariant for this grammar. It does **not** refute the representation-reset strategy.

The complexity argument only requires a fixed polynomial envelope, not equality to the initial clause count.

## Definition

Let the input encoding length be `N`. Before execution, choose a deterministic envelope

\[
B(N)=N^{O(1)}.
\]

A nonterminal projection transition is **B-reset-safe** when:

1. the current normalized state satisfies `C(F_t) <= B(N)`;
2. exact DP eliminates one existing pivot;
3. the frozen certified normalization stack is replayed;
4. no fresh variables are introduced;
5. the normalized successor is terminal or satisfies
   \[
   C(F_{t+1})\le B(N);
   \]
6. if nonterminal, variable rank strictly decreases:
   \[
   V(F_{t+1})<V(F_t).
   \]

No local CLV descent requirement is imposed on the selected projection.

## Lemma 1 — trajectory length

No fresh variables are introduced and every nonterminal selected projection removes at least one current variable. Therefore

\[
\boxed{T\le V_0\le N.}
\]

This bound is independent of whether clause or literal mass temporarily increases between normalized states.

## Lemma 2 — normalized representation bound

At every selected normalized nonterminal state,

\[
C(F_t)\le B(N),\qquad V(F_t)\le V_0\le N.
\]

For canonical non-tautological clauses, clause length is at most `V0`, hence

\[
\boxed{L(F_t)\le B(N)V_0= N^{O(1)}.}
\]

So every persisted state has polynomial explicit size.

## Lemma 3 — one exact-DP probe remains polynomial

From a state with at most `B(N)` clauses, a pivot has at most `B(N)` positive and `B(N)` negative parents. The number of parent pairs is at most

\[
B(N)^2.
\]

Each canonical resolvent has length at most `V0`, so the explicit forced-DP pool has polynomial literal mass bounded coarsely by

\[
O(B(N)^2 V_0).
\]

Because `B(N)` is fixed polynomial, this is polynomial in `N`.

The frozen R47M reducer/verifier stack is already required to stay within its theorem-safe polynomial envelopes; therefore one candidate probe remains polynomial.

## Lemma 4 — deterministic discovery remains polynomial

At a state with at most `V0` current variables, scan pivots in deterministic order and stop at the first certified B-reset-safe successor.

There are at most `V0` probes per state and at most `V0` selected nonterminal states, so

\[
\boxed{\text{candidate probes}\le V_0^2.}
\]

No macro-sequence enumeration is required.

## Conditional theorem

If there exists one fixed polynomial envelope `B(N)` such that every reachable B-bounded normalized nonterminal state generated by this controller admits a polynomially discoverable certified terminal or B-reset-safe pivot, then the resulting deterministic projection chain has polynomial total work.

The proof is the product of:

- at most `V0` selected nonterminal transitions;
- at most `V0` pivot probes per transition;
- polynomial explicit work per probe under the fixed envelope `B(N)`;
- polynomial certificate replay/reconstruction.

Thus the theorem-critical existence condition is

\[
\boxed{\forall F\in Reach_B,\ F\text{ nonterminal}\Rightarrow\exists v:\ C(Normalize(DP_v(F)))\le B(N)\text{ or terminal}.}
\]

## Relation to R47Y slack accounting

For a current state `F` with `C=|F|`, define envelope slack

\[
\sigma_B(F):=B(N)-C\ge0.
\]

For the R47A5 post-subsumption exact-DP gain

\[
g_v=C-|T_v|=d_v-s_v,
\]

we have the exact pre-normalization test

\[
|T_v|\le B(N)\iff g_v\ge-\sigma_B(F).
\]

Hence an all-pivot B-envelope obstruction must satisfy

\[
\boxed{g_v\le-\sigma_B(F)-1\quad\forall v}

or equivalently

\[
\boxed{s_v-d_v\ge\sigma_B(F)+1\quad\forall v.}
\]

Increasing the fixed envelope does not change the grammar or proof authority; it increases the admissible temporary clause-debt budget while retaining polynomial representation size.
