# JANUS TRUMP R48N — Constant-Width Persisted Envelope Theorem

Date: 2026-09-04

Status: **CONDITIONAL SYMBOLIC THEOREM; UNIVERSAL WIDTH-CAPPED SUCCESSOR COVERAGE REMAINS OPEN**

## Motivation

The amortized-pressure route R48B–R48L asks how much clause-count growth must be paid for by eliminated variables. R48J showed that a bound merely polynomial in the current clause count is circular.

There is a different representation route: bound the **maximum clause width of persisted normalized states**.

A canonical CNF over `V` variables with maximum clause width `W` cannot contain arbitrarily many distinct clauses. For fixed `W`, its entire clause universe is polynomial in `V`.

This note makes that route explicit.

## Lemma 1 — constant width implies a polynomial canonical clause universe

Let `F` be canonical, tautology-free, use at most `V` variables, and have maximum clause width at most `W`.

For every width `k`, there are at most

\[
2^k\binom Vk
\]

non-tautological clauses of exact width `k`: choose the `k` variables and one of two signs for each.

Therefore

\[
C(F)\le U_W(V):=\sum_{k=0}^{W}2^k\binom Vk.
\]

For one root-independent constant `W`,

\[
\boxed{U_W(V)=O(V^W).}
\]

Literal mass obeys

\[
L(F)\le W\,U_W(V)=O(V^W).
\]

Thus every persisted normalized state satisfying a constant width cap has polynomial representation size.

## Lemma 2 — one exact-DP probe from a width-capped persisted state is polynomial

Suppose the current persisted state has at most `U_W(V_0)` clauses and width at most `W`.

For a pivot `v`, exact DP considers at most

\[
p_vn_v\le C(F)^2/4=O(V_0^{2W})
\]

parent pairs.

Each resolvent has width at most

\[
(W-1)+(W-1)=2W-2.
\]

Hence the forced-DP representation has polynomially many clauses and polynomial literal mass in the root variable count when `W=O(1)`.

The existing R47B polynomial per-transition bounds then apply to canonicalization, R33, affine recognition/solve, RUP, SA-BVE closure, certificate production, and independent replay on this polynomial-sized forced representation.

The forced intermediate is allowed to have width up to `2W-2`; only the **persisted normalized successor** must reset to width at most `W` before the next projection step.

## Lemma 3 — variable count gives polynomial trajectory height

Exact DP removes the selected pivot. The frozen normalization stack introduces no fresh variables.

Therefore every nonterminal accepted projection satisfies

\[
V(F_{t+1})<V(F_t).
\]

There are at most `V_0` nonterminal persisted transitions.

If every state scans at most `V_0` current pivots in canonical order, there are at most `V_0^2` candidate probes over the entire trajectory.

## Conditional theorem — width-capped projection coverage implies polynomial time

Assume there exists one absolute constant `W` such that, for every valid 3-CNF root `F_0` and every reachable persisted normalized nonterminal state generated by the following policy, at least one polynomially discoverable certified pivot has exact-DP plus the frozen full normalization stack resulting in either:

1. a verified semantic terminal, or
2. a nonterminal normalized successor with
   - strict variable decrease,
   - no fresh variables,
   - maximum clause width at most `W`.

Then deterministic first-certified width-capped projection decides the root formula in polynomial time.

### Proof

- Semantic correctness composes through exact DP and the existing independently verified reducers.
- By Lemma 1, every persisted nonterminal state has polynomially many clauses/literals in `V_0<=N_0`.
- By Lemma 2, every candidate probe, forced intermediate, certificate, and independent verifier has polynomial work in `N_0`.
- By Lemma 3, there are at most `V_0` accepted nonterminal steps and at most `V_0^2` probes.
- When no variables remain, canonical simplification is terminal.

Therefore total deterministic work is `N_0^{O(1)}`.

## Width required for a P-time theorem

A root-independent constant width cap is sufficient.

A bound such as `W=O(log N_0)` is **not** enough for this theorem: the clause universe can be

\[
N_0^{O(\log N_0)},
\]

which is quasipolynomial rather than polynomial.

Thus the relevant target is genuinely

\[
\boxed{W=O(1)}
\]

or a stronger sparsity/compression theorem that reduces the number of represented clauses below the full width-`W` universe.
