## 5. Why the next move must be nonlocal contraction

The clause-local sound-cut dichotomy shows that a sound refinement confined to one clause and exported only through the original three Boolean variables is either TRUE3 or the original OR3.

The rank-one cut-history theorem likewise shows that individual local product cuts merely reactivate original clauses.

Hence any surviving refinement must be:
- nonlocal across multiple clauses;
- representation-changing or quotienting;
- exact under contraction and lift;
- equipped with a polynomially bounded decreasing invariant;
- non-enumerative.

This is algorithmically analogous to blossom-style methods: detect a global obstruction, contract it, solve the quotient, and lift the witness. The analogy is methodological only; direct delta-matroid applicability is not claimed.
