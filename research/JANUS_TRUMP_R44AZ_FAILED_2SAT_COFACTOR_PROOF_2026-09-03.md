# R44AZ — Failed-2SAT cofactor certificates

## Rule

For each variable `v` and value `epsilon`, simplify the cofactor `F[v=epsilon]` and retain only its clauses of width at most two. Call that subformula `B_2(F[v=epsilon])`.

Run the exact linear-time 2-SAT SCC test on this subformula.

- If it is UNSAT, then the full cofactor is UNSAT because the binary subformula is a conjunction-subformula of it.
- Therefore if exactly one side is rejected, the opposite value is an exact safe choice.
- If both sides are rejected, `F` is UNSAT.
- If neither is rejected, the rule makes no claim.

This is a polynomial sufficient certificate, not recursive search over the two full cofactors.

## A triple-rigid formula that the rule moves

Use the unsatisfiable linear 2-CNF

`B = (not x1 OR x2) AND (not x2 OR x3) AND (not x3 OR x4) AND (not x4 OR x1) AND (x1 OR x3) AND (not x2 OR not x4)`.

Introduce a fresh guard `g` and form

`F = AND_{C in B} (g OR C)`.

Before probing:

- all clauses have exact width three;
- there are no binary clauses;
- every 3-variable support occurs once, so each local support relation is one OR3 clause, whose affine hull is full `GF(2)^3`; R44AY extracts no equation;
- each support has one sign pattern, so its sign-translation stabilizer is zero; globally `H(F)={0}`.

At `g=0`, the cofactor becomes exactly `B`, which the 2-SAT SCC criterion rejects. Hence `g=1` is a certified exact safe choice. The rule therefore makes strict progress on a formula that is already fixed for R44AW, R44AX, and R44AY.

## Linear exact-3 obstruction

Let `F` be an exact-width-three linear CNF: any two different clauses share at most one variable.

Fix variable `v` and a value. A binary clause can arise only when a 3-clause containing `v` is shortened because the literal on `v` is falsified. Take two such clauses. They already share `v`; by linearity they share no other variable. After deleting `v`, their two-variable supports are disjoint.

Therefore all binary clauses generated by a one-variable cofactor have pairwise disjoint variable supports. Their conjunction is satisfiable: satisfy each binary clause independently. Consequently the width<=2 subformula of every one-variable cofactor is satisfiable, for both values of every variable.

Hence R44AZ has no trigger on any exact-width-three linear CNF.

Unsatisfiable linear 3-CNF formulas are known to exist; satisfiable ones are trivial to construct. Disjoint satisfiable padding gives arbitrarily large examples on either SAT-status side.
