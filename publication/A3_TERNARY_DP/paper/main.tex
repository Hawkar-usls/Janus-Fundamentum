\begin{abstract}
We study finite-field subspace arrangements containing $k$ distinct geometric subspace classes with arbitrary positive multiplicities. In the admitted formal model, middle occurrences of a repeated class can be removed without changing the optimum path-width: only the first and last occurrence are required. This yields an exact bottleneck dynamic program on endpoint states. If $s$ classes occur once and $r$ classes occur at least twice, the state graph has exactly $2^s3^r$ states and $s2^{s-1}3^r+2r2^s3^{r-1}$ directed transitions. After a $2^k$ subset-rank preprocessing table, the combinatorial dynamic-programming phase is $O(k3^k)$. The project evidence includes independent brute-force controls and tamper tests. Literature novelty remains an N3 candidate and is not externally established.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Path-width of subspace arrangements appears in the structural study of trellis representations and matroid algorithms. The purpose of this paper is deliberately restricted: we analyze arrangements with a small number $k$ of distinct geometric subspaces but arbitrary repetition. The central observation is that repetition admits endpoint compression, after which exact optimization is naturally expressed as a ternary-state bottleneck dynamic program.

The result does not claim an improvement for unrestricted matroid path-width, does not overturn general hardness results, and does not claim external confirmation of historical novelty. The accompanying machine-readable publication manifest fixes the precise theorem and claim ceiling used by this draft.

\section{Related work and novelty status}
Jeong, Kim, and Oum formulate the finite-field subspace-arrangement layout problem and give a constructive fixed-parameter algorithm parameterized by the target width~\cite{JKO}. Kashyap relates represented-matroid path-width to linear-code trellis-width and proves NP-hardness of the unrestricted represented-matroid path-width problem~\cite{KashyapPW}. Hlin\v{e}n\'y gives a self-reduction for constructing optimal matroid path-decompositions from a width decision procedure~\cite{Hlineny}. Kante, Kim, Kwon, and Oum study obstruction bounds and algorithmic consequences for bounded matroid path-width~\cite{Kante}. Choi, Korhonen, and Oum give recent faster represented-matroid branch-width/path-width algorithms based on matrix-representation machinery~\cite{Choi}.

The trellis literature also contains distinct but relevant structural work on minimal tree realizations~\cite{KashyapTree}, algebraic structure and minimality of linear trellises~\cite{ContiBoston}, and a recent exact cut-state-complexity characterization via tropical factorization rank~\cite{Sheshadri}. In the declared primary-source search protocol recorded with this paper, none of these sources supplied the repeated-geometric-class endpoint-compression theorem together with the exact $2^s3^r$ endpoint-state graph and the resulting $O(k3^k)$ bottleneck dynamic program.

\section{Discussion}
The parameter $k$ is the number of distinct geometric subspace classes rather than the total number of occurrences. Thus high multiplicity does not produce factorial occurrence-order search after endpoint compression. The algorithm remains exponential in $k$, as expected for an FPT result parameterized by $k$.
