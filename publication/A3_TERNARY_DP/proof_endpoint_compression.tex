\section{Endpoint compression}

Fix an occurrence order and a class $j$.  Let $f_j$ and $\ell_j$ denote the positions of the first and last occurrence of $U_j$.  At a cut after position $i$, class $j$ contributes to the left span exactly when $f_j\le i$, and it contributes to the right span exactly when $\ell_j>i$.  Therefore
\[
 L_i=\sum_{j:f_j\le i}U_j,
 \qquad
 R_i=\sum_{j:\ell_j>i}U_j.
\]
No occurrence strictly between $f_j$ and $\ell_j$ can change either of these two predicates.  Hence deleting all middle occurrences of every class with multiplicity at least two preserves $(L_i,R_i)$ at every distinct endpoint state, and cannot create a new span pair outside that endpoint sequence.

Conversely, every legal endpoint order can be expanded to an occurrence order with the prescribed positive multiplicities by inserting the middle copies of each repeated class between its first and last endpoints.  Those inserted copies do not change the side spans.  Thus minimization over full occurrence orders and minimization over legal endpoint orders have the same optimum bottleneck width.

It follows in particular that multiplicity affects the endpoint optimization only through the dichotomy $a_j=1$ versus $a_j\ge2$.
