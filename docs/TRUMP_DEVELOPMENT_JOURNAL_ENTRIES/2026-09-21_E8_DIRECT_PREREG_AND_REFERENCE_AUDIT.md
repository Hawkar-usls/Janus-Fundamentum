clause has at most three literals. The prospective E8-D1 slot requires a
complete deterministic Decide procedure and separate proofs of soundness,
completeness, termination, and a uniform total polynomial bit-cost bound.

comparisons, deduplication, updates, termination checks, certificate discovery,
reconstruction and verification. Efficient certificate verification is not
treated as efficient certificate discovery.

guarded-elimination theorem already in this repository. Neither is silently
promoted to unrestricted polynomial SAT.

reference procedure. Its first three correctness obligations have direct
proofs; its runtime is superpolynomial on an explicit parity projection family.
The procedure is rejected as a polynomial E8 candidate before any execution.

This is an audit of a known method, not a newly discovered algorithm or a new
universal lower bound. There was no solver run, stress sweep, independent
replay, or theorem seal. E8-D1 remains empty.
